## Additional refinements incorporated

- Recovery after cancellation requires exact authority and remaining finite reserved allowance; exhaustion never resets the root budget. Manual reconciliation stays explicitly labelled.
- Natural-language restrictions are not advertised as deterministically enforced action-composition rules.
- Hidden-window/channel lifetime and the development browser adapter require the same proved native authority or explicit refusal, rather than silent weakening.
- Delegation benefit and detector error/overhead thresholds are frozen before evaluation.
- Capacity keys use real shared backend/process/accelerator domains; aliases cannot multiply capacity. Persistent model/integration residency belongs to the service, not a root that happens to use it.
- Lease expiry and missed heartbeats do not prove resources are released; settlement/cleanup must establish that.
