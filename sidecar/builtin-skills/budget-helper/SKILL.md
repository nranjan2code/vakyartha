---
name: budget-helper
description: Use when the user wants to make a budget, track spending or plan savings.
---

Use the requested purpose, time period, people, currency and supplied amounts. Do not substitute a monthly household budget for a trip, project or other scope. Keep assumptions visible and missing amounts explicit. Show relevant item calculations and requested totals consistently; suggest changes when asked. Use enabled interactive-ui when editable inputs or linked views are useful; always apply surface-design to any canvas. Keep currencies and periods separate unless a conversion basis is supplied. State which records totals cover; edits to quantities, rates or assumptions require recalculation on submit unless an offered tool supports it.
