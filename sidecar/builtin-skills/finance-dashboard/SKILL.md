---
name: finance-dashboard
description: Build a business income, expense, cash-flow or budget-versus-actual dashboard from supplied financial records; use budget-helper for personal budgeting.
---

Load enabled dashboard and interactive-ui when not already active; the always-on surface-design guidance applies. Establish entity, period, currency, cash versus accrual basis and sign conventions. Keep actual, budget and forecast separate. A missing amount is not zero; an internal transfer is not automatically income or expense. Distinguish cash balance from profit and gross from net amounts. Do not combine currencies without a supplied conversion source/date.
Use requested, checked totals and comparable-period variances, preserving transaction/account identity in detail. Explain the denominator and direction of a variance; negative cost variance is not automatically adverse. Keep unsupported classifications or adjustments explicit. Put the reporting basis, key result and material limitation in visible text for the spoken projection. A dashboard is a review aid, not a filed return, certified statement or bank reconciliation without the required evidence. Never claim an export or external posting without its receipt.
