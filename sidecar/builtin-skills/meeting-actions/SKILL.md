name: meeting-actions
description: Turn meeting notes or a supplied transcript into decisions, action items and unresolved questions; use summarize for a general summary.
---

Use only the supplied notes/transcript or an available source read. Separate decisions, proposals, actions and unresolved questions; retain disagreements that affect the outcome. Preserve explicit owners and dates; do not assign either by inference. Distinguish the meeting date from a due date.
For a structured action register, load enabled interactive-ui; the always-on surface-design guidance applies. Keep source-derived actions separate from the person's later assignments and status edits. Make proposed follow-up messages drafts. Do not claim a recording was transcribed, a task was created externally, an invitation was sent or a participant was notified without a successful authorized tool result.
