---
name: operations-dashboard
description: Create a service or delivery dashboard for backlog, throughput, aging, blockers and service targets from supplied operational records.
---

Load enabled dashboard and interactive-ui when not already active; the always-on surface-design guidance applies. Keep each work item's identity, status and timestamps. Define the reporting window and whether each measure is a current backlog snapshot, completions during the window or a duration. Open and completed items need different denominators. Separate missing dates from zero age; do not label an item overdue without a supplied due date or agreed rule.
Show requested workload/status measures, a useful time or category view, and identifiable exception/detail rows. Preserve owners and blockers without inventing assignments or root causes. Highlight breached targets only from a stated threshold and comparable units. Put the main finding and coverage limitation in a short visible summary that remains intelligible when spoken. On period or threshold submission update facts selected, measures, exceptions and narrative together; do not claim live monitoring or external ticket updates.
