---
name: sales-dashboard
description: Create a sales performance or opportunity-pipeline dashboard with stages, targets, period comparisons and source-backed deal detail.
---

Load enabled dashboard and interactive-ui when not already active; the always-on surface-design guidance applies. Establish the reporting window, currency, stage definitions and supplied target. Distinguish booked revenue, recognized revenue and open pipeline; never combine them as one actual. Do not invent win probabilities or treat pipeline as guaranteed revenue. Use weighted forecasts only with supplied probabilities and checked calculations.
Preserve opportunity/customer identities and distinguish created, expected-close and actual-close dates. Compare equivalent windows and disclose incomplete periods. Conversion needs an explicit eligible population; percentages cannot be summed. Present requested performance measures, a relevant stage/time view and deal detail, with a brief spoken-readable finding and missing-data caveat. Submitted filters must revise measures and interpretation together. Sending outreach or changing customer records requires matching authorized tools.
