# Email & Message Drafting

Structured guidance for composing, revising and adapting professional emails, announcements and messages.

## Overview & Purpose

Use this skill when drafting new emails, replying to email threads, improving drafts, or adapting messages for specific stakeholders. Focus on clear intent, appropriate tone and actionable outcomes.

## Core Workflow

1. **Clarify objective & context**: Identify who the recipient is, their relationship to the sender, the primary message goal and any hard constraints (deadlines, sensitive topics, previous agreements).
2. **Craft the subject line**:
   - Make it specific, descriptive and actionable (e.g. `[Decision Needed] Q3 Project Budget Approval by Friday`).
   - For replies, preserve the existing subject convention unless a topic change warrants a fresh thread.
3. **Structure the body**:
   - **Lead with the point**: State the primary purpose, request or conclusion in the opening 1–2 sentences.
   - **Provide essential context**: Group supporting background, constraints or rationale into concise paragraphs or bullet points. Avoid burying key facts in dense text.
   - **Define clear next steps**: State exactly who needs to do what, by when, and what response is expected.
4. **Review & refine**: Check that tone matches the audience, names and technical terms are accurate, and extraneous details are pruned.

## Tone & Audience Calibration

- **Executive / Leadership**: Concise, outcome-focused, highlighting decisions needed, business impact and timeline.
- **Collaborative / Team**: Clear, supportive, assigning explicit ownership and sharing relevant context.
- **External / Client**: Professional, polite, clear on deliverables and commitments.
- **Sensitive / Difficult**: Objective, calm, separating documented facts from interpretations, without passive-aggressive phrasing.

## Safety & Delivery Boundaries

- **Draft by default**: Always generate email text as a candidate draft for the user's review.
- **No unauthorized transmission**: Sending an email requires an explicit user command and an authorized integration tool. Never claim an email was dispatched without a verified tool execution receipt.
- **No invented details**: Do not invent email addresses, recipient names, attachment contents or historical thread exchanges not supplied by the user.
